## Problem Statement

1. **Same-Track Self-Competition Risk (`Q-015`)**: NEAR Hackathon 2026 (`https://nearhackathon.com/`, deadline 2026-11-14 23:59 UTC) allows each participant to submit up to 2 independent projects (`Q-015`, disclosed in both READMEs). Because our 1st submitted entry (`GhostShift`, `/Users/linhancheng/Desktop/projects/near-ghost-shift`) already entered the 6 AI/security tracks (`security`, `swarms`, `agents`, `business`, `privacy`, `open`), submitting a 2nd entry in those same tracks would pit our two submissions against each other for the same track podiums (`[user: "那跟我自己有撞嗎？這場黑客松有不同主題或方向嗎？想嘗試不同的主題得獎機率比較高，或甚至可以拿兩個獎"]`).
2. **Crowded Example Clones vs. Blue-Ocean Gameplay in `games` & `nfts`**: Across existing NEAR Hackathon repositories (`created:>2026-10-15`), 6+ entries in `games` copied the literal pixel market-town merchant prompt (`09-games.md`) and 12+ entries in `nfts` copied the literal wallet-wrapping membership pass prompt (`10-nfts.md`), whereas `0` entries model an orbital power-grid and life-support survival economy where equipment NFTs carry operating reserves, physical charge, spatial zone routing, and irreversible wear (`[evidence: scratchpad/near-blueocean-ideas-synthesis.md]`).
3. **B2B Table Fatigue & Wallet-Popup Friction**: Many blockchain hackathon games fail human and AI judge evaluation (`Q-017`) because they either look like static admin tables (`[user: "遊戲我很有興趣啊，但我擔心你做不出來，或是做的不夠漂亮"]`) or interrupt the 30-second game loop with wallet popups and gas prompts (`[evidence: nearai/near-hackathon-prompt-examples tracks/09-games.md]`).

## Solution

Build **`Afterlight`** (`/Users/linhancheng/Desktop/projects/near-afterlight`, public repo `https://github.com/GGGODLIN/near-afterlight`), an interactive turn-based orbital station power-grid and life-support survival economy game on NEAR entering strictly `tracks: ["games", "nfts"]` (`[user: "1a吧，但這是個遊戲？"]`, `[user: "a"]`):

1. **3-Zone Orbital Cross-Section Tactical Game Loop**:
   - The station spans 3 interconnected spatial zones across 14 half-orbit turns (7 orbital days, alternating **Daylight** and **Eclipse**):
     - **Zone 1 — Solar Array (`SOLAR_ARRAY`)**: Houses `gen-solar-1` (`Solar Generator NFT`, generating solar power during Daylight turns and `0 kWh` during Eclipse turns, degraded by `wearBps`).
     - **Zone 2 — Transmission Corridor (`TRANSMISSION_CORRIDOR`)**: Enforces a hard physical transmission bottleneck (`corridorCapKwh = 45 kWh/turn`) between the Solar Array and the Habitat Dome. Power generated in `SOLAR_ARRAY` that exceeds `corridorCapKwh` + local battery charging is curtailed (`curtailedKwh`).
     - **Zone 3 — Habitat & O2 Dome (`HABITAT_DOME`)**: Houses `o2-scrubber-1` (`O2 Scrubber NFT`) and colonists requiring `habitatBaseLoadKwh = 20 kWh` + `scrubberLoadKwh = 15 kWh` (`35 kWh` total per turn) to maintain `100 O2 units`. Two mobile storage batteries (`bat-alpha-1` and `bat-beta-2`, each `48 kWh` base capacity) can be routed between `SOLAR_ARRAY` and `HABITAT_DOME`.
   - Why the corridor bottleneck + eclipse creates an immediate puzzle: During an **Eclipse** turn, `gen-solar-1` produces `0 kWh`. Even if `bat-alpha-1` and `bat-beta-2` are fully charged inside `SOLAR_ARRAY`, or if a single battery is left behind, trying to push peak recovery power + O2 scrubber load across the corridor hits `corridorCapKwh` or starves `o2-scrubber-1` if electricity price is set too high (`SCRUBBER_SOLVENCY_COLLAPSE`) or maintenance reserve retention is `0 bps` (`UNMAINTAINED_CAPACITY_DECAY`). Players must pre-position batteries into `HABITAT_DOME` before Eclipse and balance price vs. maintenance reserve to survive all 14 turns.
2. **Dynamic `NEP-171` Equipment NFTs & Self-Contained NEAR Rust Contract (`contracts/afterlight/src/lib.rs`)**:
   - Built on `near-sdk = "=5.29.0"` using a single `#[near(contract_state)]` struct (without `near-contract-standards` deprecated macros or `1 yoctoNEAR` deposit panics) implementing `NEP-171` (`nft_token`, `nft_tokens_for_owner`, `nft_transfer`) alongside an internal integer Station Credit ledger (`credits`) and canonical turn execution (`advance_turn`, `route_battery`, `repair_equipment`).
   - Each `EquipmentNft` (`gen-solar-1`, `bat-alpha-1`, `bat-beta-2`, `o2-scrubber-1`) stores dynamic on-chain state (`ownerId`, `zone`, `chargeKwh`, `maxCapacityKwh`, `wearBps`, `reserveCredits`, `cumulativeEarnedCredits`, `handoverHistory`). Transferring an NFT transfers future revenue control and locked `reserveCredits` to the new owner while strictly preserving `wearBps`, `chargeKwh`, and `zone` (`P3` Non-Laundering Invariance).
3. **0-Popup `NEP-366` Ephemeral Session Guard & 60-Second AI Judge Verification (`npm run verify:judge`)**:
   - Gameplay runs with 0 wallet popups via an ephemeral session key signing `NEP-366` `SignedDelegate` meta-transaction envelopes (`verifyAndExecuteNep366Delegate`, enforcing `attachedDepositYocto === 0`, `maxBlockHeight`, monotonic `nonce`, and `effectivePredecessorId === senderId`).
   - `npm run verify:judge` finishes in `< 3 seconds` (`node --experimental-strip-types`), verifying Rust contract parity, 200 `fast-check` (`fc.check()`) randomized 14-turn trajectories against an $O(L \cdot M)$ incremental algebraic Oracle (`P1..P4`) with 5 mandatory reachability witness floors, 3预设 7-day trajectories, and `P3` wash-wear exploit rejection.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Single Self-Contained Rust Contract (`contracts/afterlight/src/lib.rs`, `near-sdk = "=5.29.0"`)**:
  - Pin `near-sdk = "=5.29.0"` (exact version pin to prevent Cargo resolving to `5.30.1`), `serde = { version = "1.0", features = ["derive"] }`, and `serde_json = "1.0"`. Do not depend on `near-contract-standards` or `near-sdk-contract-tools`, and do not enable `near-sdk/unit-testing` `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 2 & Seat 4]`.
  - Implement `#[near(contract_state)] pub struct AfterlightContract` with `#[serde(rename_all = "camelCase")]` data structures, exposing `NEP-171` methods (`nft_token`, `nft_tokens_for_owner`, `nft_transfer`), station credit queries (`ft_balance_of`, `ft_total_supply`), and gameplay actions (`route_battery`, `repair_equipment`, `advance_turn`, `execute_nep366_delegate`). Extract the 6-step integer turn math into pure Rust helper functions tested via `RUSTFLAGS="--cfg clippy" cargo test` against `src/scenarios/golden-turn-vectors.json` `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 2 & Seat 6]`.
- **Canonical 6-Step Integer Basis-Point (`bps: 0..10_000`) Turn Pipeline (`src/engine/afterlight-engine.ts`)**:
  - Zero floating-point math in state transitions; all percentages are integer basis points (`0..10_000`) with `Math.trunc((value * bps) / 10_000)` and explicit remainder conservation `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 1 & Seat 6]`.
  - Fixed 4-unit initial `NEP-171` fleet (`gen-solar-1` in `SOLAR_ARRAY`, `bat-alpha-1` and `bat-beta-2` movable between `SOLAR_ARRAY` and `HABITAT_DOME`, `o2-scrubber-1` in `HABITAT_DOME`) and fixed initial total station credits (`initialTotalCredits = 10_000 CR`, distributed across `operatorA`, `operatorB`, `colonistDemandPool`, `stationTreasury`, and individual NFT `reserveCredits`). Creating or transferring to an account never mints free credits `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 1]`.
  - Every `AdvanceTurn` executes the exact 6-step pipeline and emits a structured `TurnAccountingReceipt`:
    1. **Step 1 — Solar Generation**: On odd turns (`1, 3, ..., 13`, `orbitalPhase = "DAYLIGHT"`), `gen-solar-1` produces `solarGeneratedKwh = Math.trunc((baseSolarKwh * (10_000 - genWearBps)) / 10_000)`. On even turns (`2, 4, ..., 14`, `orbitalPhase = "ECLIPSE"`), `solarGeneratedKwh = 0`.
    2. **Step 2 — Corridor Transmission & Battery Charge/Discharge**: Habitat + O2 Scrubber demand is `requiredLoadKwh = 35 kWh` (`20 kWh` habitat + `15 kWh` scrubber). Batteries currently in `HABITAT_DOME` discharge locally up to their available `chargeKwh` (capped by effective capacity after `wearBps`) without consuming `corridorCapKwh`. Any remaining demand in `HABITAT_DOME` draws from `SOLAR_ARRAY` (solar generation + batteries in `SOLAR_ARRAY`) clamped by `corridorCapKwh = 45 kWh`. During `DAYLIGHT`, surplus solar power charges batteries in `SOLAR_ARRAY` first, then batteries in `HABITAT_DOME` up to remaining `corridorCapKwh`; any unabsorbable excess is recorded as `curtailedKwh`.
    3. **Step 3 — Solvency & O2 Scrubber Settlement**: Power delivered to `HABITAT_DOME` is billed at `gridPricePerKwh` (`2`, `4`, or `8 CR/kWh`). `colonistDemandPool` pays for habitat power and O2; `o2-scrubber-1` pays for its `15 kWh` electricity input from its `reserveCredits` + colonist O2 revenue. If `gridPricePerKwh === 8` (`SURGE`) drains `o2-scrubber-1` or `colonistDemandPool` below required payment, scrubber power is cut, triggering `SCRUBBER_SOLVENCY_COLLAPSE`.
    4. **Step 4 — Revenue Split & Maintenance Reserve Retention**: Gross power revenue is split using exact integer arithmetic: `retainedToReserve = Math.trunc((grossRevenue * maintenanceReserveBps) / 10_000)` added to the supplying NFTs' `reserveCredits`, and `ownerPayout = grossRevenue - retainedToReserve` credited to each supplying NFT's current `ownerId` (`retainedToReserve + ownerPayout === grossRevenue`).
    5. **Step 5 — Wear Progression & Auto-Maintenance**: Active equipment accrues operational wear (`+600 bps` per turn). If an NFT's `reserveCredits >= autoRepairCostCredits` and `maintenanceReserveBps > 0`, it automatically spends `autoRepairCostCredits` back into `stationTreasury` (closed-loop currency conservation) to repair `500 bps` of wear; if starved (`maintenanceReserveBps === 0` or insufficient reserve), wear accumulates until effective capacity drops below survival threshold (`UNMAINTAINED_CAPACITY_DECAY`).
    6. **Step 6 — Vital Signs & Collapse Autopsy Verdict**: Compute station O2 (`0..100`) and power deficit (`unmetLoadKwh`). If `unmetLoadKwh > 0` or `o2Level <= 0`, set `stationStatus = "BLACKOUT"` with deterministic `collapseRootCause` (`"CORRIDOR_BOTTLENECK_BLACKOUT" | "SCRUBBER_SOLVENCY_COLLAPSE" | "UNMAINTAINED_CAPACITY_DECAY"`).
- **Two-Layer `NEP-366` Ephemeral Session Guard (`src/engine/nep366-session-guard.ts`)**:
  - Validates `SignedDelegate` meta-transaction envelopes in two distinct layers: `envelopeVerdict` (`Valid | InvalidDelegateSignature | StaleDelegateNonce | ExpiredMaxBlockHeight | WrongReceiverContract | NonZeroAttachedDepositForbidden | DisallowedDelegateActionKind`) and `executionVerdict` (`effectivePredecessorId === senderId`, ensuring relayer `signerId` cannot hijack a player's `NEP-171` NFT) `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 2 & Seat 5]`.
- **Single-Source Constants, Parametric Fast-Forward & Film Snapshot Export (`src/engine/constants.ts`, `src/scenarios/station-trajectories.ts`, `src/scenarios/export-film-snapshot.ts`)**:
  - Centralize grid/equipment constants (`STATION_ZONES`, `GRID_PRICE_TIERS`, `BATTERY_BASE_CAPACITY_KWH = 48`, `DEFAULT_CORRIDOR_CAP_KWH = 45`, etc.) in `src/engine/constants.ts`, and lock `tracks: ["games", "nfts"]` (`readonly ["games", "nfts"]`) directly on the `buildFilmSnapshot()` and `runJudgeAudit()` return payloads (no separate `AFTERLIGHT_TRACKS` constant in `constants.ts`).
  - Export `simulateOrbitWeek(initialState, schedule, presetMeta?)` and 3 pre-built 14-turn trajectories (`01-greedy-surge-collapse`, `02-corridor-bottleneck-blackout`, `03-balanced-survival-walkthrough`) plus `buildFilmSnapshot()` so both the `Opus 5.5 Max` Next.js UI worker and the `Opus 5.5 Max` `onetake` film worker consume engine-computed receipts directly without hardcoding formulas `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 2 & Seat 6]`.
- **Tactical Cross-Section Game UI & Judge Telemetry Drawer (`app/`, delegated to `Opus 5.5 Max` Herdr worker)**:
  - Main stage presents the orbital station cross-section SVG/Canvas (Solar Array Wing, Animated Transmission Corridor with load meter `kWh / 45 kWh`, Habitat & O2 Dome with live window lights & O2 gauge, orbital Day/Eclipse arc, Web Audio synthesized tactical sound effects, 3 discrete turn controls, 1-click 7-day fast-forward playback, and Single Root-Cause Blackout Autopsy Banner) `[user: "遊戲我很有興趣啊，但我擔心你做不出來，或是做的不夠漂亮"]`.
  - Equipment NFT rack displays live wear/charge/reserve state with 1-click **`Handover NFT to Operator B`** and **`Simulate Exploit: Wash Wear / Mid-Payout Siphon`** buttons `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 3]`.
  - Collapsible **`Judge & On-Chain Inspector`** drawer exposes `NEP-171` token state, `NEP-366` `SignedDelegate` receipts, `P1..P4` `fast-check` counters, and a **`Run Judge Check`** button calling `GET /api/verify` `[evidence: .scratch/afterlight/brainstorm-review-2026-11-08.md Seat 3]`.

## Testing Decisions

- **What makes a good test**: Test observable behavior, conservation identities, and rejection error codes exclusively through the 3 agreed public seams (`src/engine/*`, `src/verifier/judge-audit-runner.ts`, and `app/api/{simulate,verify}/route.ts`). Zero floating-point approximations; zero internal mocks.
- **Modules under test**:
  1. `src/engine/afterlight-engine.ts`, `src/engine/afterlight-oracle.ts`, `src/engine/nep366-session-guard.ts`, and `contracts/afterlight/src/lib.rs` (via `tests/afterlight-engine.test.ts` and `RUSTFLAGS="--cfg clippy" cargo test`).
  2. `src/verifier/judge-audit-runner.ts` and `src/scenarios/station-trajectories.ts` (via `tests/judge-verifier.test.ts`).
  3. `app/api/simulate/route.ts`, `app/api/verify/route.ts`, and `README.md` judge contract (via `tests/api-routes.test.ts`).
- **Conditional PBT Specification (Shape 1 — Multi-Step Interlocking State Machine & Conservation Invariants)**:
  - **Applicability domain**: Turn-scheduled action sequences (`14` orbital turns per run, `numRuns: 200`, `seed: 20261108`) interleaving `SetPolicyAction` (`gridPriceTier`, `maintenanceReserveBps`), `RouteBatteryAction`, `TransferEquipmentNftAction` (valid owner handover, wash-wear attempt, non-owner/relayer hijack attempt), `RepairEquipmentAction`, and `AdvanceTurnAction`.
  - **Mapped Invariants (`P1..P4`) checked after every single step**:
    - **`P1` (Closed-Loop Currency Conservation)**: After every action step, `operatorACredits + operatorBCredits + colonistDemandPoolCredits + stationTreasuryCredits + sum(nft.reserveCredits) === initialTotalCredits` (`10_000 CR`), all balances are non-negative safe integers, and revenue split satisfies `retainedToReserve + ownerPayout === grossRevenue`.
    - **`P2` (Energy & O2 Balance Conservation)**: On every `AdvanceTurn` step, `receipt.solarGeneratedKwh + receipt.batteryDischargedKwh === receipt.habitatConsumedKwh + receipt.scrubberConsumedKwh + receipt.batteryChargedKwh + receipt.corridorLossKwh + receipt.curtailedKwh`, and no battery's `chargeKwh` exceeds its wear-adjusted effective capacity `Math.trunc((maxCapacityKwh * (10_000 - wearBps)) / 10_000)`.
    - **`P3` (`NEP-171` Transfer Non-Laundering & Anti-Sybil Invariance)**: Executing `TransferEquipmentNft(tokenId, from, to)` changes `ownerId` to `to` while strictly preserving `wearBps`, `chargeKwh`, `reserveCredits`, and `zone` (`nextNft.wearBps === prevNft.wearBps && nextNft.reserveCredits === prevNft.reserveCredits && nextNft.chargeKwh === prevNft.chargeKwh`). Non-owner or relayer-signer transfer attempts are rejected with `UnauthorizedNftTransfer` with zero state mutation. Furthermore, splitting ownership of the 4 equipment NFTs across 1 vs. 2 operator accounts under identical turn policies yields identical station energy output, wear progression, and total payout (`totalOperatorPayout_1Account === operatorAPayout_2Accounts + operatorBPayout_2Accounts`).
    - **`P4` (Transmission Corridor Capacity Cap & Terminal Blackout Finality)**: On every turn, `0 <= receipt.corridorFlowKwh <= state.corridorCapKwh` (`45 kWh`). Once `stationStatus === "BLACKOUT"`, `collapseRootCause` is non-null and immutable unless an explicit reset/new-game action is issued.
  - **Independent Oracle Boundary (`src/engine/afterlight-oracle.ts`)**:
    - Implemented as an $O(M)$ step-level algebraic conservation & shadow-ledger verifier (`evaluateAfterlightOracleStep`) that checks `P1..P4` without importing or calling the engine's internal turn-resolution helper.
    - Uses `fc.check()` to emit structured `firstDivergence` diagnostics (`turnIndex`, `orbitalPhase`, `violatedInvariants`, `ledgerDiff`) on any failure, and enforces 5 aggregate Reachability Witness floors across the 200-run batch (`completed14TurnRunsCount >= 40`, `eclipseBatteryDischargeTurnsCount >= 150`, `corridorCapClampedTurnsCount >= 40`, `degradedNftTransferPreservedCount >= 40`, `maintenanceStarvationTurnsCount >= 40`).
    - Role 3 calibration: Includes individual test-local mutations for `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, and `P4` proving each invariant independently flips to `false` when violated (without adding any fault-injection flag to production code).
